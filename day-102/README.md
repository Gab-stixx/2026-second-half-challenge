# Day 102/184 — Software Engineering: Passion vs Discipline

## Overview

Today's focus was a controversial discussion about software engineering: **Do you need to be passionate about coding to become a good software engineer?**

## My Thoughts

I believe passion can help, but it isn't the only thing that matters. You don't have to code all day or make programming your entire personality to become a good engineer.

Consistency, curiosity, discipline, and a willingness to keep learning are also important, especially because technology keeps evolving.

There's a difference between being committed to your craft and making it your entire life.

## Key Takeaway

You can take software engineering seriously without being obsessed with coding. What matters is continuing to learn, solving problems, and building useful things.

## Discussion Question

Can someone become an excellent software engineer without being passionate about coding?
